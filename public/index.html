1. IoT Edge Sensing Layer

2. Peak Prediction & AI Decision Engine

Detects late-afternoon solar decline and forecasts upcoming 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM demand spikes. Generates non-disruptive recommendations (chiller setpoint adjustments, hostel water heater staggering, decorative lighting trim).

3. Zero-Disruption Protection

Ensures mission-critical research laboratory equipment (cryo-freezers, fume hoods, lasers) and university server clusters are hardwired with unbreakable safety locks, completely protected from load-shedding actions.
